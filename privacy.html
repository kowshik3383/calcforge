Privacy

Calculator inputs

Calculations run in your browser and this site does not transmit the values you enter.

Contact drafts

Contact feedback stays in the current page until you download it or close the page. The form does not send a message to CalcForge.

Newsletter preview

The newsletter preview saves your email in this browser’s local storage. It does not send email. Use “Clear saved email” in the footer or clear site data in your browser to remove it.

Hosting

A hosting provider may record requests to serve the site. This version includes no third-party analytics.
