Built for practical work.

CalcForge is a toolbox for growers, tradespeople, and anyone planning a hands-on project. It starts with two common questions. How much ground do I have, and how much material should I buy?

Show the working

Each calculator explains the units and formula behind the result so you can check it against your measurements. The calculations run in your browser and you can use them without creating an account.

Start with the real dimensions

Use the guides to decide what to measure and where an estimate needs more information. For irregular spaces, break the plan into smaller shapes before adding their areas.
