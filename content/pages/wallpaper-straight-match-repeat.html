Estimate wallpaper with a straight pattern repeat

Include straight-match pattern alignment in the length of each full drop.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Add trim allowance to the wall height. Round that length up to a whole pattern repeat for a simple straight-match estimate, then divide the usable roll length by that cut length and round down.

Worked example

A 2.38 m wall plus 0.10 m trim gives 2.48 m. With a 0.32 m repeat, budget 2.56 m per drop. A 10 m roll yields three drops.

What to check

Allow separately for starting alignment. Offset matches need a product-specific plan.
