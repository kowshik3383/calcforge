Convert square inches to square feet

Roll up small repeated parts into a square-foot materials total.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Divide square inches by 144. If there are several identical pieces, multiply by count before rounding the final square-foot result.

Worked example

Twelve 24 in² pieces total 288 in², or 2 ft².

What to check

Avoid dividing area by 12. A square foot contains 12 inches along each of two sides.
