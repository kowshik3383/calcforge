Subtract tracks and ponds from plantable area

Find usable field area after excluding features that can't be planted.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Draw exclusions inside the outer boundary and give each a label. Subtract only their portions inside the measured boundary and count any shared area once.

Worked example

A 9,000 m² field with a 420 m² pond and a separate 180 m² track leaves 8,400 m².

What to check

Keep gross and net figures together so later estimates use the right denominator.

Use the field calculator for the rectangular footprint, then apply this guide’s method to the result.
