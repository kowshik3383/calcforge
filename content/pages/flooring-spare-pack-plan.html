Separate installation waste from future repair stock

Show the purchase impact of keeping a sealed box for future repairs.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Find installation-only boxes using expected cuts. Then add your spare inventory as whole boxes on a separate line so the reserve remains intact.

Worked example

If 8 boxes are needed for installation and one sealed spare is planned, buy 9; the repair reserve is 1 box.

What to check

Record the product name and batch information before the packaging is discarded.

Use the material calculator when the product is sold by area per pack.
