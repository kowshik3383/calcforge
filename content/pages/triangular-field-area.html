Calculate the area of a triangular field

Find the area of a triangular plot using the base and perpendicular height.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Choose one side as the base. Measure the shortest distance from the opposite corner to the line through that side, then multiply by the base and divide by two.

Worked example

A 54 m base and 32 m perpendicular height give 864 m², or 0.0864 ha.

What to check

On an obtuse triangle the perpendicular may meet the extension of the base outside the plot.
