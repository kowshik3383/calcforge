Measure sprinkler output with catch cans

Record how much water a sprinkler deposits where it actually lands.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Distribute identical straight-sided containers around one watering zone. Run the sprinkler for a timed interval and measure water depth in each container.

Worked example

Readings of 4, 5, 5, and 6 mm after 15 minutes average 5 mm, or about 20 mm per hour under the observed conditions.

What to check

Keep the range as well as the mean. Repeat if wind or overlapping zones distort the result.
