Estimate planting positions on a rectangular grid

Count positions along each axis before multiplying to get a grid total.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Mark the usable edge margins, then count the number of centre-to-centre intervals that fit across and along the bed. Each axis has one more position than complete intervals.

Worked example

With 20 cm margins, a 160 × 100 cm bed at 30 cm grid spacing has 5 × 3 = 15 positions.

What to check

Remove blocked positions individually for permanent supports or irregular bed edges.
