Estimate packs of decorative wall panels

Convert the measured wall surface to packs and check the actual panel layout.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Subtract untiled areas and use the pack’s effective coverage. For slatted or interlocking panels, verify the field width once seams overlap.

Worked example

At 1.8 m² of effective coverage per pack, 7.6 m² requires five packs before any extra allowance.

What to check

Short leftovers cannot always move between courses. Sketch cuts around fixed openings.

Use the material calculator when the product is sold by area per pack.
