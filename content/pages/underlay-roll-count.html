Estimate flooring underlay rolls

Use roll coverage as a first pass and check your strip layout before ordering.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Divide net floor area by coverage per roll, then plan the actual widths and seams. Count extra strips where the layout prevents reusing leftovers.

Worked example

A 24 m² room at 10 m² per roll needs at least three rolls, assuming enough usable strips can be cut.

What to check

Check product instructions for seam treatment. Overlap, tape, and direction can affect the ordering list.
