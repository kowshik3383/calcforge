Build a garden water use log by zone

Organise run times and estimated litres so you can compare watering sessions.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

For each zone record the date, run minutes, measured rate, and any rain observation as separate columns. Multiply that zone’s duration by its own rate.

Worked example

One zone at 8 L/min for 9 minutes uses 72 L; another at 5 L/min for 11 minutes uses 55 L.

What to check

Keep monitored flow and rainfall as separate fields. Mixing them conceals what you actually supplied.
