Record spare stock and returns when ordering packs

Keep future repairs and surplus returns visible in the purchase plan.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

List installation packs, planned spares, and returnable surplus as separate lines. Note return deadlines and batch or shade identifiers where applicable.

Worked example

An order of ten packs with eight planned for installation and one reserved for repair leaves one pack potentially available for return.

What to check

Check supplier policy and actual unused stock before counting a refund in the budget.
