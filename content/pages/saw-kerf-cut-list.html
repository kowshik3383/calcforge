Allow for saw kerf when planning repeated cuts

Include the blade’s removed width in a simple stock-length plan.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Add part lengths and the kerf of each separating cut. Also include any waste from squaring an end. Measure the actual kerf if fit is tight.

Worked example

Four 290 mm parts with four 3 mm cuts occupy 1,172 mm of a 1,200 mm blank before end trimming.

What to check

The number of cuts depends on whether a stock end is accepted as a finished edge.
