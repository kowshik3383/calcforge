Size a workbench mat around a fixed fixture

Choose a mat by cuttable dimensions and keep fixture openings in the sketch.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Measure the full mat footprint, then mark any cutout and edge clearance. Use the full cut preform when deciding what roll or sheet fits.

Worked example

A 1.8 × 0.7 m mat with a 0.2 × 0.15 m cutout has 1.23 m² of installed surface but still needs a blank large enough for the full outline.

What to check

Check whether useful sections can be cut from joined pieces or whether a single uninterrupted mat is required.
