Read flooring coverage labels before ordering

Turn the area per box printed by the supplier into a box count.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Use the pack’s stated area rather than the area of one board. Convert area units so that both the room estimate and box coverage use the same square unit.

Worked example

An adjusted requirement of 18.6 m² at 2.2 m² per box calls for nine boxes.

What to check

Check the label for the exact size and product code. Similar boards can ship with different pack coverage.

Use the material calculator when the product is sold by area per pack.
