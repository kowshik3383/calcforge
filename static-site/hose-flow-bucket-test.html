Measure hose flow with a bucket and timer

Find litres per minute under the same conditions as a planned watering job.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Fill a container to a known volume and record elapsed seconds. Multiply the litres by 60 and divide by seconds; use the same tap opening and attachment each time.

Worked example

Filling 10 L in 25 seconds gives 24 L/min.

What to check

A test with the nozzle removed won't describe flow through a nozzle. Repeat at the working setup.
