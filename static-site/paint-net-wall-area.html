Calculate net wall area for paint

Measure walls and deduct large openings from the painting area.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Multiply each wall’s width by its height. Add the wall areas and subtract surfaces left unpainted, such as doors and windows. Track trim independently if it takes a different finish.

Worked example

Four walls in a 4 × 3 m room with 2.5 m ceilings total 35 m². Deducting a 2 m² door and a 1.5 m² window leaves 31.5 m².

What to check

If wall height changes, split the wall into manageable shapes instead of using the highest point for everything.
