Find side length from a square plot area

Convert an area target into the side length of an equivalent square.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Convert the area to the square of the desired length unit, then take its square root. For an actual rectangle, use the measured side lengths instead.

Worked example

A square covering 625 m² has sides of 25 m. A 20 m by 31.25 m rectangle covers the same area.

What to check

Area alone gives no unique shape. An equivalent square won't tell you the real perimeter.

Use the field calculator for the rectangular footprint, then apply this guide’s method to the result.
