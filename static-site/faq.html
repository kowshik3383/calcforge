Good questions.

Are the calculators free?

Yes. Both calculators are free to use without an account.

Where do I find the formula?

Open “Formula and example” beneath the result in each calculator.

Can I calculate an irregular field?

Split the field into shapes you can measure separately. Add their areas using the same unit. The field calculator handles each rectangular portion.

Do you store my measurements?

The calculations run in the current browser tab. The site does not save or send your measurements.

Can I use a result for a purchase?

Use it as a planning estimate. Confirm your measurements, pack coverage, and installation requirements before placing an order.
