Estimate area along a curved field boundary

Use evenly spaced offsets to turn an irregular boundary into a rough area estimate.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Lay out a straight reference line inside the plot. At regular stations, record the total width perpendicular to the line. Multiply each interval length by the mean of its two widths and add the strips.

Worked example

Widths of 10, 14, and 12 m at 5 m intervals yield 60 + 65 = 125 m² for the measured stretch.

What to check

Use closer stations where the edge bends sharply. Note which ends of the plot the strip estimate excludes.
