Estimate fill for a round planter

Use the inside radius and fill depth for a straight-sided circular container.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Divide the measured inside diameter by two. For vertical sides, multiply π by radius squared and by the fill depth. For a tapered pot, this is only an upper estimate if you used the widest radius.

Worked example

A straight-sided planter 40 cm across and 30 cm deep holds about 38 L at that depth.

What to check

Check whether a reservoir, false bottom, or unfilled rim reduces the soil depth.
