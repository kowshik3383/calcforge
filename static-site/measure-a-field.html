How to measure a rectangular field

Turn two measured sides into an area you can compare across units.

Measure both sides

Measure the length and width along adjacent edges of the same rectangle. Record both in metres. On a 120 m by 80 m plot, the area is 9,600 square metres.

Convert to hectares

Divide square metres by 10,000. The same field covers 0.96 ha. Keep the original measurements alongside the result so you can reproduce it later.

Handle a bend in the boundary

Sketch the outline and split it into simple shapes. Add the component areas using the same unit. For a legal boundary or a purchase based on land area, use the official survey.
