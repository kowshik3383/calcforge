Estimate paint litres for two coats

Translate net wall area and label coverage into an estimate for multiple coats.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Multiply the net area by coats, then divide by the paint label’s coverage in m²/L. Price primer separately when it has different coverage.

Worked example

Two coats on 34 m² at 11 m²/L require about 6.18 L before container rounding.

What to check

If the stated coverage is a range, run both ends to find the purchasing range.
