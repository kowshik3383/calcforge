Start with these featured calculators, or browse the full catalog below. Use them for estimating area and planning a materials order.

Field area

For a rectangular plot. Enter both sides in metres.

Your area will appear here.
Formula and example

Length × width gives square metres. Divide by 10,000 for hectares or by 4,046.8564224 for acres. A 120 m × 80 m plot is 9,600 m², or 0.96 ha.

Material estimate

Use the actual floor area and coverage printed on one pack.

Your pack estimate will appear here.
Formula and example

Multiply the floor area by (1 + waste ÷ 100), divide by pack coverage, then round up to a whole pack. For 24 m², 10% allowance, and 2.2 m² per pack, the estimate is 12 packs.
