Make a supplier quote easy to compare

Collect the pack, delivery, and product details that change a material quote.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Use a table with supplier, product ID, coverage, pack count, pack price, delivery, and return terms. Ask suppliers to quote the same specification and unit basis.

Worked example

Nine ₹900 packs plus ₹500 delivery total ₹8,600. Eight ₹1,000 packs plus ₹200 delivery total ₹8,200 if both meet the same coverage need.

What to check

If the specifications differ, compare fit and coverage before selecting the lower total.
