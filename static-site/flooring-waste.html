WORKSHOP

Plan for flooring cuts and waste

Estimate pack counts using the layout and coverage on the product label.

Start with the rooms

Sketch each room and add the areas to find the total. Measure alcoves separately if adding them makes the plan easier to check.

Choose an allowance

Look at the pattern and room layout. A diagonal layout or many short cuts can leave fewer reusable offcuts. Check the manufacturer’s installation guidance before setting a waste percentage.

Round once

Apply the allowance to the total area, then divide by pack coverage. Order whole packs. For example, 26.4 m² of adjusted area at 2.2 m² per pack is 12 packs.
