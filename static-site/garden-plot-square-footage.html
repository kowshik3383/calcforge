Find square footage for a garden plot

Measure the bed footprint in feet and use area units for the result.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Convert inches to fractions of a foot before multiplying. For example, 6 inches is 0.5 ft. Keep a separate row for each bed if dimensions differ.

Worked example

A bed measuring 8 ft 6 in by 3 ft 6 in covers 8.5 × 3.5 = 29.75 ft².

What to check

Do not enter feet and inches as decimal digits. A length of 8 ft 6 in is not 8.6 ft.

Use the field calculator for the rectangular footprint, then apply this guide’s method to the result.
