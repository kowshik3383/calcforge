Understand why area conversion factors are squared

Avoid applying a length factor directly to a two-dimensional measurement.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

If one unit of length equals a factor times another, square the factor to convert an area. Cube it to convert a volume.

Worked example

A 30 cm square has area 900 cm². Converting the sides first gives 0.30 × 0.30 = 0.09 m².

What to check

Use the unit label as a dimensional check. Linear, square, and cubic units have different factors.
