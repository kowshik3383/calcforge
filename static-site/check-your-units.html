PLANNING

A unit check before you calculate

Catch mismatched units before they distort an estimate.

Write the unit beside every value

Record each value as you measure it. A length of 250 cm is 2.5 m. Convert lengths to the same unit before multiplying.

Keep length and area separate

Metres describe one dimension. Square metres describe two. A 2.5 m by 4 m room covers 10 m².

Round only at the end

Carry the measured values through the calculation. Round the display for readability and round pack counts up when you need whole packs.
