Convert feet and inches to decimal feet

Write a mixed-unit tape reading as the decimal needed for area calculations.

By CalcForge · 21 November 2026

Method

Divide the inches by 12, then add the whole feet. Keep extra precision if you need to multiply two measurements later.

Worked example

11 ft 9 in becomes 11.75 ft, and 11 ft 3 in becomes 11.25 ft.

What to check

Entering 11.9 in a calculator means 11.9 ft, or 11 ft 10.8 in, if the input expects decimal feet.
